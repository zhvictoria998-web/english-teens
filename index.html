Change it

Make the sentence negative, or change the time as shown.

  1. I went camping in July. → I in July.
  2. She catches the bus every day. → She every day.
  3. We spent a lot of time on our phones. → We on our phones.
  4. Every day I get up at 7. (yesterday) → Yesterday I at 7.
  5. Last summer we stayed at home. (every summer) → Every summer we .
  6. Does she read a lot? (in summer) → a lot in summer?
