The worst first day ever

Last September Zoe moved to a new town with her family, and her first day at her new school was a disaster. First, her alarm didn't ring, so she got up forty minutes late. She grabbed a banana, put on the first clothes she found and ran to the bus stop. Of course, she missed the bus.

When she finally arrived, the class was doing a maths test. The teacher smiled and gave her a test paper too. Zoe was so embarrassed that her face went red, and she couldn't answer a single question.

At lunch she couldn't find the canteen, so she sat alone on a bench in the playground and ate a bag of crunchy crisps. While she was eating, a girl with a pink backpack sat down next to her. "Hi, I'm Emma," she said. "Your hoodie is so cool. It really suits you!" They started chatting, and it turned out that they lived in the same street.

After school the girls walked home together. Zoe felt relieved: the day was terrible, but now she had a new friend. Today, a year later, Emma and Zoe are still best friends, and every Friday they have a sleepover at Emma's house or at Zoe's.
